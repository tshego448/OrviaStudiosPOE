# Website Data-Handling Policy

**Owner:** Orvia Studio Group — Information Officer to be named and registered  
**Scope:** Public website, contact/service-enquiry messages, and associated email handling  
**Version:** 1.0 — 6 October 2026

This policy sets the operating controls for the current static website. It is not a legal opinion. The business owner must complete the launch checks at the end, and the policy must be reviewed if the data flow or providers change.

## 1. Data inventory and purpose

| Data | Source | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name and email | Contact or enquiry form | Identify the sender and reply | Yes |
| Message type or enquiry type and message | Contact or enquiry form | Understand and respond to the request | Yes |
| Phone | Contact or enquiry form | Optional alternative contact route | No |
| Budget/contribution range and preferred date | Service-enquiry form | Tailor cost/availability guidance | No |
| Privacy-notice acknowledgement | Contact or enquiry form | Record that the notice was acknowledged | Yes in the UI; it is not a blanket consent for unrelated uses |
| IP address, requested URL, browser/device metadata, request time | Web-host connection logs | Service delivery, abuse prevention, and security | Generated by the host, not requested by the form |
| Theme preference | Browser local storage | Remember light/dark appearance on that device | Optional site convenience; not sent to Orvia by site code |

The forms must not request passwords, identity numbers, payment-card data, health details, or other sensitive information. If a sender includes such data unexpectedly, do not copy it into other systems; restrict access and delete it when no longer needed for the enquiry or a legal obligation.

## 2. Current data flow

The site is static. No form endpoint or website database is configured. Browser validation runs locally. Submitting the form creates a prefilled `mailto:` draft; it does not send the message. The visitor's chosen email application/provider receives the draft contents and may sync/store the draft. Orvia receives the information only after the visitor sends it to `orviacreatives@gmail.com`.

The contact page displays a static, locally hosted SVG map made from public-domain Natural Earth boundaries. Viewing it does not call a remote map/tile provider; it shows country-level coverage, not an office or street address. Social links require an explicit click. The current site code contains no analytics or advertising integration.

The actual static-host provider and log-retention configuration are deployment-specific and have not been verified from this repository. The Orvia address is a Gmail address; the active account owner, account controls, and provider settings must be confirmed by the business.

## 3. Access, mailbox, and device controls

1. Assign named people who need to handle enquiries; grant access only to those people and remove access promptly when roles change.
2. Enable multi-factor authentication (prefer passkeys/security keys where supported) on the Orvia email account. Secure recovery methods and review signed-in sessions and forwarding rules.
3. Use company-controlled, supported devices with screen locks, current security updates, and device encryption. Do not forward enquiry messages to personal accounts or paste them into unapproved tools.
4. Avoid including unnecessary personal details in replies, subject lines, screenshots, analytics, or logs. Do not log full request bodies, message text, or email drafts.
5. Review mailbox delegates, forwarding, connected applications, and recovery contacts at least quarterly and after any personnel change.

These controls require action in the email and device accounts; editing the website cannot turn MFA on or verify mailbox security.

## 4. Retention and deletion

- **Unconverted enquiries:** review at closure and delete or de-identify no later than 12 months after the last meaningful contact unless there is a documented continuing business purpose, dispute, or legal requirement.
- **Active projects and transaction records:** retain only under the applicable approved client, contract, accounting, and legal record schedule; do not apply the enquiry period blindly to records subject to another rule.
- **Unsent drafts:** Orvia does not receive or control drafts left in a visitor's email application. Explain this in the public notice; do not claim the business can delete those drafts.
- **Host logs and backups:** obtain the provider's retention and deletion terms, record them in the vendor register, and use the shortest period consistent with delivery, security, and legal requirements.

The current site cannot automatically purge mailbox records. The Information Officer or delegated mailbox owner must perform and record a quarterly review sufficient to meet the schedule above. If the business cannot operationally meet this schedule, revise the notice and policy before launch rather than promise automatic deletion.

## 5. Provider and operator review

Before a provider receives personal information on Orvia's behalf, record its service, data categories, processing location, subprocessors, retention/deletion, security controls, incident contacts, and cross-border safeguards. Put the required confidentiality and security terms in a written operator agreement. Review hosting, email, backup, form, analytics, and customer-support tools; do not add a provider merely for convenience.

No external form endpoint is currently configured. Before adding one:

- approve the vendor, purposes, data location, retention, and operator terms;
- require HTTPS and server-side allowlist validation, field/size limits, output encoding, and safe error handling;
- add rate limiting and anti-abuse controls; use CSRF protection if the design uses authenticated sessions/cookies;
- restrict staff access, protect stored data and backups, keep secrets server-side, and exclude message contents from logs;
- update the public notice and allow only that endpoint's origin in the Content Security Policy `connect-src` directive;
- test deletion, access controls, incident reporting, and failure behavior before enabling the endpoint.

Browser-side checks improve usability but are not a security boundary; the server must validate all submitted fields.

## 6. Data-subject requests

Route privacy requests to `orviacreatives@gmail.com` and notify the Information Officer. Verify identity proportionately before disclosing or changing records, locate relevant mailbox/provider records, assess applicable rights and exceptions, record the response, and correct/delete/restrict records where required. Do not disclose another person's information in a response. Keep the Regulator's contact and escalation details available to the Information Officer.

## 7. Security-incident response

If there is a suspected loss, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction involving personal information:

1. Notify the Information Officer/Deputy Information Officer immediately and preserve relevant evidence without circulating message contents unnecessarily.
2. Contain the event: revoke exposed credentials, suspend affected integrations, secure accounts/systems, and contact the relevant operator. An operator must alert Orvia immediately under the written terms.
3. Record what happened, when it was discovered, affected information/people, systems/providers, containment, and remaining risks. Do not wait for a complete investigation before escalation.
4. Where there are reasonable grounds to believe information was accessed/acquired by an unauthorised person, the responsible party must notify the Information Regulator and affected data subjects as soon as reasonably possible, subject to the Act's provisions. Follow current Regulator guidance, which does not provide a low-risk exemption; do not wait for a complete investigation. Give affected people practical steps to reduce harm and update the notice as facts become available.
5. Correct the weakness, document lessons, and review safeguards. Use the Regulator's current eServices process and current guidance.

The Information Officer owns the decision and regulator communication; website code cannot perform the organisation's statutory notifications.

## 8. Website and deployment controls

- Keep only necessary fields; phone, budget, and preferred date remain optional.
- Keep the email-draft disclosure and privacy link adjacent to each form.
- The site uses a same-origin Content Security Policy and self-hosts the Satoshi WOFF2 font. Any external form endpoint, script, stylesheet, font, frame, image, or analytics integration requires a security/privacy review and a narrow CSP update.
- Force HTTPS at the actual host. The `_headers` rules in this project are Netlify-style and are not applied by every host; verify headers on the live deployment. HSTS is included without `includeSubDomains`; enable only after every intended hostname is HTTPS-ready.
- Keep dependencies minimal and updated. Review the privacy notice when the host, form flow, email account, map behavior, or third-party resources change.
